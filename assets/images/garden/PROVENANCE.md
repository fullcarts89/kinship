# Garden art: provenance

The Electric Garden's botanicals (FD-30b). Every image was generated for Kinship through the
image-generation connector attached to the workspace, on 19–20 October 2026, from text prompts written
for Kinship, with no reference image from the mood boards or from any third party. Nothing is traced,
cropped or reused from the boards. The record below is copied from the creative reset's
`02-techniques-and-assets.md` (branch `claude/kinship-creative-reset`, Stage 1); the prompts are in that
session's transcript and can be re-issued.

The pieces were resampled for the app (sips, Lanczos) from the Stage 1 originals; nothing else changed.
