# Notebook botanicals

The seven single flowers (anemone, fern, iris, peony, poppy, ranunculus, tulip) are the identity
flowers of the Moment Deck × Notebook prototype (PR #77, `docs/product/explorations/moment-deck-notebook-2026-11/img/b-*.png`),
reused there from PR #76. They were generated for Kinship on 31 Oct 2026 through the workspace image
connector; the job ids are recorded in PR #76's README. Nothing here is traced from a third party.

They are identity only (D8, contract §6): a person's flower is chosen from their id alone and never
grows, fades or changes with history. 420 × 420 px, transparent.
