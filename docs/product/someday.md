## Launch and growth (free downloads, no ad spend)

Both reels below come from one creator's series, "Things I set up for every app launch to get
free downloads". Saved 2026-11-13 with the note: "Something to remember if Kinship ever becomes
something."

### Part 4: a search-answer page for each problem the app solves

[Instagram reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdF7KNkx3jF/), 48 s.

- The creator says they get over 50,000 downloads a month without spending on marketing.
- People search Google for problems the app already solves, but the app doesn't show up in
  the results. Give each problem its own small, working page on the website. The page answers
  the question, then links to the app.
- The reel's example is a bill-splitting app with a "how to split rent fairly" calculator page
  that leads into the app.
- To check demand before building a page, use Google Ads' **Keyword Planner**. It shows related
  searches with monthly volume estimates, filterable by country, and works without running ads.
- Their workflow: brainstorm how people would search for what the app does, check those
  searches in Keyword Planner, then build a quick page for each of the top results.

### Part 5: get listed on comparison sites that AI assistants read

[Instagram reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdYdT_gR142/), 37 s.

- Many people now ask AI assistants for app recommendations. Those assistants draw on
  established sources, and comparison sites ("best X alternatives") are among them.
- Some comparison sites let you list your app as an alternative to a well-known app. The
  assistant then finds your app next to a name it already knows, along with a reason to
  recommend it.
- **AlternativeTo** and **SaaSHub** take free submissions.
- The GitHub list `alvinunreal/awesome-submitlist` collects 300+ places to submit a launch:
  directories, launch sites, newsletters, communities, subreddits and marketplaces.

### If this is ever picked up for Kinship

Every public page or listing must follow the same rules as the app. That means no AI badges or
AI framing, no relationship scores or "health", and no example people or memories that read
like real users' data. This needs founder review before anything goes live.
