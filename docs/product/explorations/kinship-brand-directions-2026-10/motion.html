The rule each concept obeys

  1. Understood answers "did it hear me?", once, in the direction's own mark.
  2. Arrival says "something is here for you", under 600 ms, and never again on the same open.
  3. A celebration happens for the friend's day, not for the user's action, and never for a hard day.
  4. Yes is an acknowledgment in words with a small nod. Nothing is scored.
  5. Into a person keeps the mark continuous so the page feels like the same person.
  6. Press is tactile feedback, 2 px, nothing more.
