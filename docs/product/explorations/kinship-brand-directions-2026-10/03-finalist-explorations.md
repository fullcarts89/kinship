# 03 · Finalist explorations: Woven, Garden Letter, Bloom

**Status: EXPLORATORY · UNAPPROVED.** Three directions developed across the whole product in `prototype.html` (press *Finalists (A · B · F)* to see them side by side on any scenario). Each has Today (every state), People (15, 110, search), the portrait (six people), the first Tell, the hand-off and return, a sensitive scenario, a quiet state, light and dark, Reduce Motion, larger text and iPhone SE, and the sheets (detail, correction, clarification, a long Tell, kept + pending, an Ask result, a planning idea). Items marked Proposed / Alpha / Future are not built and not approved.

## 1. The shared skeleton (unchanged in all three)

Everything below is identical in the three finalists and is the Garden Letter's experience architecture carried forward: the two-item bar; the Tell dock pinned above it; Today as date · greeting · one Moment (kicker, statement, context, paraphrase, provenance, the person) · one primary action · Not now · This week (≤ 3 lines and "N more"); People search-first and alphabetical with an A–Z index past 30; the portrait as name · relationship and place · the now line · Lately · Coming up · You said you'd · Between you · Message / Call / Tell; the detail sheet with "From your note"; the hand-off sheet with "You could mention" and the sentence that Kinship sends nothing; the return question with one observed fact, Yes / Not yet and "Open Messages again"; Resolve; the optional "Anything worth remembering?"; the quiet day with the season line; the Ask row and typed results (provisional); the plain rule for a hard day.

## 2. F · Woven, in full

**The system.** Linen ground, ink text, teal for "understood" and "you said you'd", the floss palette only inside bands and charms. Gabarito 700 statements (36/40), 600 lines (19/26), Instrument Serif italic for kickers, greetings and verbatim words, Instrument Sans for everything the machine says. Hairline rows, 26 pt gutter, pills h/2. The brand band (coral · saffron · teal chevron) under the active tab and on a quiet day; a person's band on their Moment (120 × 12), their row (56 × 10) and their page (full width, 18, with knots).

**Today.** A Moment is the letter with the person's band under the provenance; the kind of day is the kicker plus, for a celebration only, charms on a thread at the top right (a flag bead for a race the day before, a star for a birthday, two rings for a wedding, a knot for a promise). A hard day: no kicker, no charms, the band stays (it is identity, not mood). The quiet day: the season line, "Nothing needs you today.", the brand band, the week. First use: the worked example as a small letter; the first Tell (Proposed, PR #52 concept B) as one person, one story, with "Someone else ▾" and the person's band beside it.

**People.** Bands instead of avatars; names in Gabarito 600; the line beneath in sans. At 110 the screen is a quiet rainbow of equal-width bands and reads instantly as "these are mine". Search filters only; the Ask row sits under results when there is a query (AR-2).

**The portrait.** The band tied across the top of the page, then the approved portrait. Lena (one memory): the band and one line do not look sparse. Sam (nothing yet): the band makes an empty page feel like a person already. Nana (remembered): the band at 55% and the one sentence, never on Today. Mom (sensitive): plain, no "idea", no charms.

**The hand-off and return.** The sheet is the approved sheet. Back from Messages: "Did you wish Ben luck?" with "You opened Messages earlier." and the band; Yes → "You wished Ben luck." in place and the band gives one small nod (4°, 480 ms; nothing under Reduce Motion). The optional prompt follows.

**A sensitive situation.** Mom's surgery: the statement, the context, the source, the band, Message Mom, Not now. Nothing else on the page is coloured except the band and the teal dot. The hand-off sheet offers no "You could mention" when the hand-off is sensitive (K26's rules, unchanged).

**Light and dark.** Charcoal ground; the floss unchanged; pills invert.

**Scale.** iPhone SE: the statement at 32/36 and the charms at 96 keep the Moment, its actions and one week line above the dock. Larger text ×1.3: the band keeps its height; the charms move above the statement at AX sizes (as the Garden Letter's art does).

**The voice variable (F′).** The prototype's *serif voice* toggle sets Newsreader for statements, names and lines while keeping Gabarito for controls. It is the Garden Letter's voice with Woven's identity. We prefer Gabarito (friendlier, more contemporary, still adult) and expect older cohorts to prefer Newsreader; the study decides.

## 3. A · Garden Letter, in full

The corrected round-2 design, exactly, with the new scenarios added: the first Tell (Proposed), the long Tell and kept + pending, Which Sam?, the Ask result and the planning idea (Future) on the same paper, the same sheet, the same serif. Nothing was weakened; the prototype's Garden phones use round 2's sprig output and engravings. Its strengths and limits are in `00` §2 and `02` §1.

## 4. B · Bloom, in full

Cream ground, ink text and borders, poppy for "understood". Fraunces statements with the soft and wonk axes on; DM Sans for the machine. A colour field per kind of day behind the Moment (race cornflower tint, birthday pink, wedding marigold, promise mint) with flat occasion art at 148 px; no field and no art on a hard day; a petal drift once on a birthday. A flat flower for every person. People at 110 is the direction's best screen (bright, equal, legible). The portrait: a big flower beside the name, then the approved sections in Fraunces 500. Dark: an aubergine ground with darkened fields and unchanged flowers. Its strengths and limits: `02` §1.

## 5. What the finalists have in common that the other three did not

All three keep the Garden Letter's *skeleton* intact and change only the *skin* (colour, type, marks, art) and *temperature* (how much joy is shown). That is deliberate: the brief's category of changes that this exploration concerns is the visual layer. Nothing in any finalist changes an entry point, a navigation treatment, a hierarchy, a control, or a behaviour.
