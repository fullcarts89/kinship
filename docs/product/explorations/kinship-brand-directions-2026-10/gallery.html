Six directions for Kinship

EXPLORATORY · UNAPPROVED. The same product, the same fictional people, the same four states in every direction: a Moment (Ben's race tomorrow), a person (Priya), a birthday (Maya) and a hard day (Mom's surgery). The phones are live: click the words, the pills, the rows. Nothing here is built, approved or scheduled.

The question each direction has to answer: does it make Kinship an app that celebrates having people in your life, that someone is happy to open, that is recognisable without its logo, and that is still mature enough to hold a sick friend's week?
