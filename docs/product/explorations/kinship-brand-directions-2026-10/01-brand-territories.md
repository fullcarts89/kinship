# 01 · Brand territories: six directions

**Status: EXPLORATORY · UNAPPROVED.** Six distinct creative concepts, each specified far enough to be a plausible production application, each rendered on the same product behaviour and the same fictional content (`gallery.html`, `prototype.html`). The specification data lives in `specs.js` and is the source for the gallery; this document adds the reasoning. Hex values are proposals; every text pair is ink on ground at 4.5:1 or better.

Common to all six (see `00` §5): the approved experience architecture; identity-only marks at one size per place; one drawing per kind of reason and none for a hard day; one meaning for the "understood" colour; the quotation rule; Resolve after Yes; a 150 ms Reduce Motion twin for everything; Lucide icons for functions only, always labelled.

---

## A · Garden Letter (the baseline)

The corrected round-2 design, included unweakened. **Personality:** calm, intimate, trustworthy. **Metaphor:** a letter about one person, read in a quiet garden. **Colour:** paper `#EFEEE9`, ink `#1D221E`, ink-quiet `#5E645D`, ochre `#A87A22` (understood, your promise), brick `#9A3B2A`; night garden `#121513` / `#E6E4DC` / `#D9B25A`. **Type:** Newsreader Display 36/40 for the statement, Newsreader 19/26 for lines, italic kickers, Instrument Sans for the machine. **Illustration:** one-ink engravings; the sprig; the meadow; the sundial; the hour's tint. **Shape:** hairline rows, no cards, sheets at 28, pills h/2, 26 pt gutter. **Navigation:** two words with a hairline underline; the Tell field pinned above. **Night:** the night garden. **Interaction:** press dims; the words lift 2 pt (provisional). **Motion:** arrive 320 ms / 8 pt; understand 600 ms; Resolve. **Accessibility:** complete (approved). **Scale:** sparse and rich proved in round 2 (15 and 110 people, six portraits). **App icon:** a hairline sprig on paper. **Complexity:** low; M–L of view work already sized. **Risks:** reads as serious or literary; the garden around the sprig reads as a wellness or journaling product; low memorability for younger users; a palette that stops at ink.

## B · Bloom (the electric garden)

**Personality:** alive, warm, generous: the friend who turns up with flowers. **Metaphor:** the garden in full colour, drawn flat and bold. **What it keeps from A:** the letter composition, the kicker, the quotation rule, the plain rule, open rows on People and the portrait. **What it changes:** colour fields behind each kind of day (race `#DDE3FF`, birthday `#FFE0EE`, wedding `#FFEFBF`, promise `#DDF5E6`; nothing for a hard day), flat three-colour occasion art (a wreath of leaves, a cake, two rings, a bow) at 148 px, a flat flower for every person (5–8 petals, three petal shapes, two colours from the id; the same radius for everyone, forever), 2 pt ink borders on fields and controls, and a soft, slightly wonky display serif.

**Colour:** cream `#FFF6E5`, ink `#1A1A1A`, poppy `#FF4D2E` (understood), marigold `#FFB300`, cornflower `#3D5AFE`, pink `#FF7AB6`, leaf `#1F8A4C`, lilac `#B48CFF`; night `#141019` with fields `#23264A` / `#44213A` / `#4A3A14` / `#1C3D2C`. **Type:** Fraunces (SOFT 100, WONK 1) 700 at 40/42 for statements and 46 for names; Fraunces 500 opsz 24 for lines; DM Sans for the machine, buttons at 700. **Iconography:** Lucide at 2.0 in ink, never coloured. **Illustration:** flat, no outlines, three colours per drawing (Duolingo's craft rule, none of its mechanics); a seeded petal drift for a celebration only. **Shape:** fields at 26 radius bleeding to the edges; chunky 52 pt pills. **Navigation:** two bold words, a 4 pt poppy underline; a bordered Tell field. **Light/dark:** the fields darken, the flowers keep their colours. **Interaction:** firm, tactile, slightly springy (no overshoot on text). **Motion:** the flower *unfolds* on arrival (420 ms, scale .6 → 1, −10° → 0); one petal drift on a birthday; Resolve. **Accessibility:** text never sits on a saturated colour; fields are tints with ink text at ≥ 7:1; petals are decorative. **Scale:** the fields hold up to a three-line statement; past that the art drops to 104 px; People at 110 with 40 px flowers reads as a bright list and is the direction's best screen. **App icon:** one flat flower, cream on poppy. **Complexity:** medium (an illustration set, fields, variable-font instances in React Native). **Risks:** juvenile or greeting-card; colour fatigue; long statements fight fields; a pink field under a surgery is a trust failure, so the plain rule must be enforced in code. **Vitality is prohibited:** nothing wilts, grows, brightens or counts.

## C · Keepsake (the memory scrapbook)

**Personality:** personal, tactile, a little messy: the friend who keeps your ticket stubs. **Metaphor:** a box of things kept. **The idea:** each memory is a slip on a kraft desk; the user's own verbatim words, and only those, are in handwriting; provenance is typewritten; people are hand-cut stickers (circle, scallop, tilted square, burst; a washi colour and the initial from the id); tape at the corners; a seeded doodle per kind of reason. A hard day is a straight, plain card: no tape, no doodle, no tilt.

**Colour:** kraft `#E9DCC5`, card `#FFFCF5`, ink `#2B2622`, red pen `#D23F31` (understood), mint `#BFE3D0`, butter `#FBE8A6`, blush `#F6C9C9`, sky `#C9DDF2`; night desk `#1E1A16`, slips `#2A2521`. **Type:** Lora 600 at 32/38 for statements, 38 for names; Caveat for kickers, greetings and verbatim words; Work Sans for body; Courier Prime for labels and provenance. **Iconography:** Lucide 1.8; a pencil for Tell. **Illustration:** 2.2 pt ink doodles with a seeded jitter (balloon, finish flag, rings, bow); tape; torn edges; stickers. **Shape:** slips at 3–4 px radius with a paper shadow and ±1.2° tilt; dashed rules; index-tab navigation. **Light/dark:** a dark desk with warm slips; tape desaturated. **Interaction:** things are picked up and put down. **Motion:** slips drop and settle (360 ms) then tape fades in; the doodle draws itself on a birthday; Resolve. **Accessibility:** tilt removed under Reduce Motion and at AX sizes; handwriting never below 20 pt; typewriter labels at 11 pt are the weak point and should be 12. **Scale:** a rich portrait becomes a stack of slips (the gallery's Priya shows it holding at three per section; at AX1 it does not); 110 people with stickers is lively and legible. **App icon:** a scalloped sticker with a K. **Complexity:** medium–high (tilts, shadows, textures, a handwriting face, density rules). **Risks:** decorative chaos; "school project"; long text on tilted slips; the handwriting face must stay restricted to verbatim words or it cheapens everything.

## D · Orbit (connected worlds)

**Personality:** contemporary, lively, clear. **Metaphor:** overlapping circles: the places where two lives touch. **The idea:** no botany. Three translucent discs for every person (one of four fixed arrangements, three colours from the id, the same diameter for everyone, never a graph and never an edge), a big soft disc bleeding off the corner of each screen coloured by the kind of day (none on a hard day), white cards on lavender, a geometric display face.

**Colour:** lavender `#F4F3FF`, indigo `#15153A`, violet `#5B49F2` (primary, understood), coral `#FF5C7A`, aqua `#34E0D0`, amber `#FFB547`, lime `#C6FF3D`; night `#0E0F23`, cards `#181A36`. **Type:** Syne 700 at 31/36 for statements, 42 for names; Manrope 600 for lines, 500 for body. **Iconography:** Lucide 1.8; a ring for provenance. **Illustration:** geometry only: discs, rings, arcs; multiply blends in light, screen in night. **Shape:** 16–20 radius cards, no borders, 14 px soft shadows; a segmented pill nav. **Light/dark:** the strongest night mode of the six. **Interaction:** smooth, weightless. **Motion:** discs converge on arrival (480 ms); rings expand once on a birthday; Resolve. **Accessibility:** blends degrade to flat fills on devices without support; nothing depends on hue alone. **Scale:** cards make 110 people tall but tidy; the portrait is the most legible of the six. **App icon:** three discs on indigo. **Complexity:** medium (blend modes need care in React Native; otherwise simple shapes). **Risks:** reads as fintech or a productivity tool; the least human of the six; discs invite closeness readings that have to be designed out; a hard day looks like a settings screen.

## E · Postmark (postcards, places and shared experiences)

**Personality:** warm, social, outward: the friend who sends postcards and shows up to things. **Metaphor:** a postcard from a friend's week. **The idea:** every Moment is a postcard: a flat poster for the kind of day on top, the message in a book serif beneath, a postmark with the date, a perforated stamp for the person (seven glyphs: sun, mountain, wave, tree, house, moon, bird; two colours from the id; one size). A hard day is a plain card with no poster and no colour; the postmark stays, because a date is just a date.

**Colour:** offwhite `#F7F1E3`, navy `#1E2A3A`, poster red `#C4301F` (primary, understood), sea `#2A6FB8`, mustard `#E8B23A`, forest `#2E6B4F`, salmon `#F2A28B`; night `#16202B`. **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque 800 (wdth 90) at 30/33 for statements, 44 for names, 700 for buttons and caps labels; Literata for lines, italic kickers. **Iconography:** Lucide 1.8 in navy; the postmark's lines. **Illustration:** flat three-colour posters; stamps; the postmark. **Shape:** 1.5 pt navy borders, 6 radius, a 4 px hard offset shadow; 6-radius buttons; uppercase nav with a red underline. **Light/dark:** navy ground, posters keep their colour, the primary goes salmon. **Interaction:** crisp, printed. **Motion:** the card slides in like post (380 ms) and the postmark stamps (220 ms); the poster wipes in on a birthday; Resolve. **Accessibility:** the postmark text is decorative (the date is in the kicker); posters are decorative. **Scale:** the card's fixed poster height is the problem: a three-line statement pushes the week off an SE; posters must be made for each new kind of reason. **App icon:** a perforated stamp with a K. **Complexity:** medium–high. **Risks:** looks like a travel or events app; heavy to maintain; a "postcard" of a surgery needs the plain rule more than any other direction.

## F · Woven (the friendship bracelet; the wild card)

**Personality:** warm, hand-made, enduring; joy without noise; the thing you never take off. **Metaphor:** a friendship bracelet for every person: a pattern you tied once, and keep. **The idea:** no garden, no postcards, no collage, no geometry. Every person gets a woven band: one of four weaves (chevron, stripe, diamond, dot) in three floss colours from their id. It is drawn at one fixed width per place (120 × 12 on Today, 56 × 10 in a row, full-width 18 on the page, with knots) and **never lengthens, frays, brightens or gains a bead for anything the user does**. The brand's own band (coral · saffron · teal chevron) marks Today and the active tab. A rounded display face carries the warmth; a serif italic carries the human lines (kickers, greetings, the user's own words). A birthday strings a few charms on a thread (a star bead; a flag bead for a race; two rings; a knot for a promise); a hard day strings none and the page is plain.

**Colour:** linen `#FBF7F0`, ink `#22201E`, teal `#2BB3A3` (understood, your promise), floss coral `#F25C54`, saffron `#F7B32B`, cobalt `#2D5BE3`, berry `#B5307A`, lime `#9BCB3C`, orange `#FF8A5C`, violet `#6A4CE0`; night `#1B1917` with the floss unchanged (it is the identity). **Type:** Gabarito 700 at 36/40 for statements, 42 for names, 600 for lines; Instrument Serif italic 20/26 for kickers and verbatim words; Instrument Sans for the machine. (F′, a serif voice with Newsreader statements, is a toggle in the prototype: it is the one typographic variable the study should settle.) **Iconography:** Lucide 1.8 in ink. **Illustration:** the bands and the charms; nothing else. **Shape:** open paper, hairline rows, pills h/2, 26 pt gutter: the approved system with one ornament. **Navigation:** two words; the brand band under the active one. **Light/dark:** warm charcoal; floss unchanged. **Interaction:** the approved press; the band is touchable on the page (it opens Edit). **Motion:** the band weaves in on arrival (520 ms, left to right); charms string on one by one for a birthday; Resolve with a small nod; Reduce Motion fades. **Accessibility:** floss triads are chosen from a palette checked for deuteranopia and protanopia separation (two of the four weaves also differ by shape, not only hue); the band is decorative to VoiceOver and the name is the label; at AX sizes the band keeps its height. **Scale:** rows at 110 are quiet and bright; the portrait is the approved portrait with a band at the top; sparse (Lena) and empty (Sam) pages work because the band is all a person needs to look present. **App icon:** a short chevron band tied in a loop. **Complexity:** low–medium (one SVG generator, six charms, a variable font; the rest is the approved system). **Risks:** craft or camp connotations; the band must never be read as length or progress (fixed width per place, by rule and by test); less illustrative than B or E.

### Considered and set aside for the wild card

- *Threshold* (painted front doors; "a door out of the app" made literal): memorable and non-botanical, but a closed door on a quiet day is cold, a row of doors is a street not a circle, and it drifts toward real estate.
- *Lantern* (a steady light per person): warm, but "lit" invites "unlit", which is exactly the vitality reading the contract forbids.
- *Marginalia* (a typography-only zine with highlighter marks): cheap and youthful, but it fails the contract's test that a screen with the signatures hidden must not belong to a notes app.
