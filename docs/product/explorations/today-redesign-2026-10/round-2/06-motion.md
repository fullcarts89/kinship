# After Yes: the motion comparison

**Status: EXPLORATORY · UNAPPROVED.** Three versions of the one moment where Today acknowledges that the user showed up, built side by side in `motion.html` (press Play on each; toggle Reduce Motion). The brief's tests: subtle, responsive, warm; never a task completed or a reward collected; nothing suggests the relationship is consumed; Reduce Motion equally coherent; no new haptics without a demonstrated benefit.

| | Original (round 1) | Settle | **Resolve (recommended)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| What moves | The whole block presses into the paper (scaleY → 0.9, 10 pt down, fade, 600 ms); a warm light passes over the top of the page (900 ms); the person's root mark in the hedgerow fades | The block drops 4 pt and quietens to 55% (320 ms); the pills and link fade; the prompt arrives below | The question cross-fades to its answer in place (240 ms): "Did you ask Ben how it went?" → "You asked Ben how it went."; the context, pills and link fade; the prompt arrives below with the approved arrive |
| Duration to rest | ~1.5 s | ~0.65 s | ~0.6 s |
| Haptic | success (approved) | success | success |
| What it says | "Done, collected, gone" | "Done, set aside" | "Yes, you did that" |
| Suggests the relationship is consumed? | Yes: the letter is crushed and vanishes | Somewhat: a greyed block lingers like a struck task | No: a sentence changes tense; nothing is crushed or struck |
| Reduce Motion | A 150 ms fade of everything at once (coherent but abrupt) | A 150 ms fade | The same cross-fade at 150 ms: identical in meaning |
| Depends on | The hedgerow (withdrawn), a gradient (decision 5) | Nothing | Nothing |
| Risk on device | Three simultaneous animations; the scale breaks "nothing scales" | Low | Low; one opacity transition and the approved arrive |

**Recommendation: Resolve.** It is the only version whose acknowledgment is in Kinship's own medium, words, and in Kinship's own voice: the question answered, nothing rewarded. It needs no new element, no new haptic, no gradient, and it is the same thing under Reduce Motion. The letter leaves only when the user answers the optional prompt ("Nothing today" or a Tell) or moves on, which is when the reason is done in the product's own terms.

**For the logistics case** (a promise's Yes, I2): the same cross-fade, then one quiet line, "That's done. Nothing else to remember from it.", and Today moves on; no prompt.

**Withdrawn:** the press (C2), the light (C3), the root mark (C1), the Not now tick and the sheet tap (D3).
