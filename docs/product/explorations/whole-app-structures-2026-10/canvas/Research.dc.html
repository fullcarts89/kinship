What the research says

About 85 sources across two passes: apps and award winners whose structure differs, and studios whose compositions are bold rather than decorated. Three findings shaped every option.

Finding 1 · the trap
Every personal CRM (Clay, Dex, Garden, Fabriq, Covve, UpHabit) is a contact list plus a cadence. The humane-looking ones (Poppy's wilting plants, Dunbar's rings, hey!'s streaks) restyle the cadence. That is exactly a reskin.
Finding 2 · one dominant object
Strong screens give each view one hero, at least half the screen: a poster, a print, a date, a card. Lists live one gesture away (Apple Photos' pinch, Weather's list). Google found that scattering things for style broke recognition, so the reading order stays.
Finding 3 · less time in app
The best precedents for Kinship's anti-engagement stance live outside the app: Flighty's Live Activities, Locket's widget as the product, grug's one finite page a day. Today can be mostly off-screen.
