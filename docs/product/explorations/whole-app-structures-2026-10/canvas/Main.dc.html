Change the object,
not the skin.

Every pass so far kept one skeleton: a search field, an A–Z list, a sprig, a name and a line. The research is blunt about why that keeps happening. Every personal CRM is a list plus a reminder cadence, and the warm-looking ones just restyle the cadence as plants that wilt or rings that shrink. Each of the five structures below changes what a screen is about.
