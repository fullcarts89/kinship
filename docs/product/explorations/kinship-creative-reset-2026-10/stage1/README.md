## The two directions in one line each

- **A · Electric Garden, reinvented.** Original grainy botanical art bigger than the page; the person in a fluid mask with a leaf crossing in front; the name at poster scale; a dawn wash for the kind of day. Without a photo the name itself is filled with the bloom.
- **B · Living Memories.** The user's own photograph, full-bleed, with its caption and source; the name split by the photograph's edge, cream above and navy below; one postmark for the date; one hard colour field. Without a photo the person's two-colour field and the occasion itself carry the screen.

## Rules kept on every screen

A visible source on every statement (From your note · Oct 8; Your caption · May 3); one primary action and Not now; the Tell field and the two-item bar on Today; nothing counted, ranked or scored; no "last contacted"; the user's words in quotation marks; no AI anywhere; nothing about Ben that is not in `fixtures.md`. Photos are the user's own choice, captioned only by the user, never inferred from, and never on a sensitive line.

**Photos in this folder are generated sample imagery; "Ben" is not a real person** (see `02-techniques-and-assets.md`).
