## What is still not good enough

**Both**

- Every screen still has the same lower third: statement, a line, a source, a pill, two week items, the Tell field, the bar. It is correct and quiet, but the board's energy stops at the headline. Stage 2 should decide whether the lower third can carry the direction (colour on the pill, a typographic rhythm for the week) without becoming a form again.
- Only one scenario is drawn. A birthday, a quiet day and a sensitive line will test both directions harder than a race does; the race is the easy case because it is joyful.
- Dark mode is not drawn. Direction A's wash and Direction B's sand paper both need their night answer before anyone believes them.
- The grain in A is uniform; a real print would have more in the shadows. Small, but it is the difference between "printed" and "filtered".

**Direction A**

- The no-photo Today reads as a poster about a name. It is complete and it is pretty, but the brief asks for the person to be the emotional centre and a name is not a person. The honest options are a stronger typographic voice for the person (their own words larger) or accepting that A is a direction that is better with photos.
- The lavender person-page bloom is more "pretty flower" and less "electric" than the hero bloom; the two pieces come from the same prompt family but the hero is the only one with the board's bite.
- One leaf asset (`a-leaf.png`) is rendered rather than printed; it should be regenerated in the corner piece's style.

**Direction B**

- The person page has room for only one fact under the prints at this density. A real page has up to twelve lines; the prints would have to become a strip, or the hero shorter. That is a Stage 2 layout question, not a visual one, but it is real.
- The caption on the photo (top-left, photo-credit style) is legible but small; on a brighter photo it would fail. A scrim or a placement on the paper is needed in Stage 2.
- The postmark is one mark too many on the no-photo Today, where the occasion is already typeset in the field.

## Where the two directions stand against each other

B is the more finished of the two at this stage, because photography does most of its work and the split headline is a single strong idea. A is the more *Kinship* of the two: it survives without a photo, it keeps the warm serif for what the user said, and its art can change with the kind of day. A needs its second and third assets to reach the hero bloom's quality; B needs its lower half to earn the top half.

Neither is a recommendation. That is the founder's call at this gate.
